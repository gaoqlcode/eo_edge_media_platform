## 5. 基础设施双路径

| 路径 | 启动 | PG | Redis |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Local | `bash scripts/start_infra_local.sh` | `:55432` | `:56379` |
| Compose | `sudo docker-compose -f platform/infra/docker-compose.yml up -d` | `:5432` | `:6379` |

E2E 默认 Local。切换 Compose 时改 `DATABASE_URL` / `REDIS_URL`。
